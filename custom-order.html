How it works

Made for your collection, dyed in our workshop

Whether you are a designer planning a seasonal line, a boutique restocking a best-seller or a family preparing for a wedding, we dye to order in any of our fabrics.

  • 01
    Share your ideaFabric, colours, pattern and quantity, plus a reference photo if you have one.
  • 02
    Approve a swatchWe dye a small sample and send photos or a physical swatch within 3–5 days.
  • 03
    We dye your orderMost orders are ready in 2–3 weeks. Bulk orders of 50m+ receive trade pricing.
