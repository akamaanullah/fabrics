Help

Frequently Asked Questions

How is fabric sold?

All fabric is sold by the meter, in half-meter steps, with a minimum of 1 meter. Your order is cut as one continuous length unless you ask us to cut separate pieces.

How long does delivery take?

Lahore orders arrive in 1–2 working days and the rest of Pakistan in 3–5 working days. Delivery is free over Rs. 5,000, otherwise a flat Rs. 250.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

Yes, cash on delivery is available nationwide. You can also pay by card, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa.

Will the colour run?

Our dyes are colour-fast, but hand-dyed fabric can release a little colour in the first wash. Wash separately in cold water and dry in shade.

Can I return fabric?

Uncut, unwashed fabric can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Custom-dyed orders are made just for you and are final sale.

What is the minimum for custom or bulk orders?

Custom orders start at 5 meters per colourway. Orders of 50 meters or more receive trade pricing. Start a request
