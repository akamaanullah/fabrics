Our Story

A decade of colour, tied by hand

Noor Fabrics began in 2014 as a small family workshop in Lahore with one indigo vat and a love for traditional bandhani. Today our artisans tie thousands of knots by hand every week, dye in small batches and dry every length in the sun.

We make fabric for designers, boutiques and anyone who wants their clothes to carry a little of the maker's hand.
